FAQs

What time should guests arrive?

Please arrive at least 30 minutes before the ceremony.

When is the RSVP deadline?

Please reply by October 5, 2026 so we can finalize our headcount.

Can I bring a plus one?

Please check your invitation in the RSVP section. If your invitation includes a plus one, the option will appear automatically.

What should I wear?

Please follow the guest color palette: Dusty Rose, Antique Rose, or Blush Mauve.

Will it be cold in Dahilayan?

Yes, it can get cool. Please bring a jacket or shawl.

Is there parking?

Free parking is available at Dahilayan Forest Park on a first-come, first-served basis.
