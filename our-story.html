Our Story

From Our First Run to Forever

A story shaped by miles, unexpected detours, faith, and choosing each other through every climb.

September 2019

How We Met

We met through a running community in Vertis North, Quezon City. Philip was one of the coaches, and Liezl was one of the trainees. Somewhere between exhausting running drills and pre-race nerves, every run started to feel like another excuse to see each other.

Road runs soon became trail runs, hiking trips, food trips, and countless adventures together. Along the way, we realized we had found the perfect teammate for life—someone ready for both the easy roads and the steep climbs.

September 2023

The Proposal

Fast-forward to our trip to Singapore. Philip planned to propose beneath the glowing Supertrees at Gardens by the Bay, but before he could ask, the park closed and the lights went completely dark.

He quickly changed course and brought Liezl to the Merlion instead. With the skyline glowing and the city lights reflecting on the water, Philip asked the question—and Liezl said yes. It was not the original plan, but it became completely ours.

Through Every Season

Faith & Us

Running is what got us moving, but our faith is what keeps us together. Sunday Mass has been our anchor from the very beginning, no matter how busy life gets.

Running taught us how to keep moving through long roads and difficult climbs. Our faith teaches us how to keep pace with each other, side by side. Now we are ready for the next journey—one we hope to walk together for the rest of our lives.

“Running brought us together. Faith keeps us together.”
